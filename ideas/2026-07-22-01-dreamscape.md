# Dreamscape
> Revolutionizing mental health therapy with immersive virtual reality experiences
## Problem
Mental health disorders are a growing concern, and traditional therapy methods can be limited in their ability to provide immersive and engaging experiences for patients. Current solutions often lack the personalization and interactivity needed to effectively treat complex conditions. This can lead to low patient engagement and limited treatment outcomes.
## Target Users
* Psychologists and therapists
* Healthcare professionals specializing in mental health
* Researchers studying the effects of VR on mental health
## Key Features
* Customizable VR environments for various mental health conditions
* Personalized therapy plans based on patient progress and feedback
* Integration with popular electronic health record (EHR) systems
* Real-time patient monitoring and feedback mechanisms
* Library of pre-built VR scenarios for common mental health conditions
* Support for user-created VR content
## Tech Stack
### Frontend
* React for building user interfaces
* A-Frame for VR rendering and scene management
### Backend
* Node.js with Express.js for server-side logic
* GraphQL API for data querying and manipulation
### Database
* MongoDB for storing patient data and therapy plans
### Infra/DevOps
* AWS for cloud infrastructure and deployment
* Docker for containerization and orchestration
### AI/ML
* TensorFlow for building predictive models and patient insights
## Architecture
The Dreamscape architecture will consist of a React-based frontend, a Node.js backend with GraphQL API, and a MongoDB database for storing patient data. The A-Frame library will be used for VR rendering and scene management, while TensorFlow will be integrated for predictive modeling and patient insights. The system will be deployed on AWS using Docker containers for scalability and reliability.
## Roadmap
* v1: Initial release with basic VR environments and therapy plans
* v2: Integration with EHR systems and support for user-created VR content
* v3: Advanced predictive modeling and patient insights using machine learning algorithms, with expanded library of pre-built VR scenarios and enhanced patient monitoring and feedback mechanisms
