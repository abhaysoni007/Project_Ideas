# Provenance
> Empowering art authenticity through transparent ownership histories
## Problem
The art market is plagued by forgery and ownership disputes, making it difficult for artists, collectors, and galleries to verify the authenticity of unique digital and physical artworks. This lack of transparency undermines trust and value in the market. As a result, there is a growing need for a reliable and secure system to track and verify ownership histories.
## Target Users
* Artists creating unique digital and physical artworks
* Collectors seeking to purchase and own authentic artworks
* Galleries and dealers facilitating art sales and exhibitions
## Key Features
* Digital certificate of authenticity generation and verification
* Blockchain-based ownership history tracking and recording
* Artificial intelligence-powered forgery detection and analysis
* User-friendly artwork registration and management interface
* Integration with popular online marketplaces and social media platforms
* Secure storage and retrieval of high-resolution artwork images and documentation
## Tech Stack
### Frontend
* React for building responsive and interactive user interfaces
* Redux for state management and data storage
### Backend
* Node.js with Express.js for building scalable and secure server-side APIs
* GraphQL for managing complex data queries and schemas
### Database
* MongoDB for storing and retrieving artwork metadata and ownership histories
* IPFS for decentralized storage of high-resolution artwork images and documentation
### Infra/DevOps
* Docker for containerization and deployment
* Kubernetes for orchestration and scaling
* AWS for cloud infrastructure and services
### AI/ML
* TensorFlow for building and training forgery detection models
* OpenCV for image processing and analysis
## Architecture
The Provenance system will utilize a microservices architecture, with separate services for frontend, backend, and AI/ML processing. The frontend will interact with the backend via RESTful APIs, while the backend will store and retrieve data from the database and IPFS storage. The AI/ML service will run in parallel, analyzing artwork images and detecting potential forgeries.
## Roadmap
* v1: Develop and launch the core platform with digital certificate of authenticity generation, blockchain-based ownership history tracking, and basic artwork registration and management features
* v2: Integrate AI/ML-powered forgery detection and analysis, and add support for popular online marketplaces and social media platforms
* v3: Expand the platform to include additional features such as secure storage and retrieval of high-resolution artwork images and documentation, and integration with physical art galleries and exhibitions
