# JobJourney
> Empowering careers, one journey at a time
## Problem
Job seekers often struggle to navigate career transitions, lacking personalized guidance and support. This can lead to prolonged unemployment, skill stagnation, and decreased job satisfaction. Effective career coaching and mentorship are essential to address these challenges.
## Target Users
* Recent graduates seeking entry-level positions
* Mid-career professionals looking to transition to new roles or industries
* Individuals re-entering the workforce after a career break
* Career changers seeking to pivot into new fields
* Entrepreneurs and freelancers seeking to expand their professional networks
## Key Features
* Personalized career assessments and goal-setting tools
* AI-driven job matching and recommendations
* Access to a network of experienced career coaches and mentors
* Interactive learning resources and skill development courses
* Job search tracking and application management tools
* Community forums for peer support and discussion
## Tech Stack
#### Frontend
* React.js with Redux for state management
* Material-UI for responsive and accessible UI components
#### Backend
* Node.js with Express.js for API routing and management
* GraphQL for querying and mutating data
#### Database
* MongoDB for storing user data and career information
* PostgreSQL for storing job listings and application data
#### Infra/DevOps
* AWS for hosting and scalability
* Docker for containerization and deployment
#### AI/ML
* TensorFlow.js for client-side machine learning and AI-driven job matching
## Architecture
The JobJourney application will follow a microservices architecture, with separate services for user authentication, career coaching, job matching, and community features. This will enable scalable and flexible development, allowing for easy integration of new features and services.
## Roadmap
* v1: Launch core platform with career assessments, job matching, and community features
* v2: Integrate AI-driven coaching and mentorship tools, with personalized learning resources and skill development courses
* v3: Expand platform to include job search tracking and application management tools, with enhanced analytics and insights for users and career coaches
