# MedMingle
> Connecting patients, amplifying hope
## Problem 
The lack of support and understanding from others who are going through similar medical conditions can be a significant challenge for patients, leading to feelings of isolation and frustration. Existing online forums often lack personalization and security, making it difficult for patients to share their experiences and connect with others who truly understand their situation. This void can hinder the healing process and overall well-being of patients.

## Target Users
* Patients diagnosed with chronic or rare medical conditions
* Caregivers and family members of patients with medical conditions
* Medical professionals seeking to provide additional support to their patients

## Key Features
* Personalized matchmaking based on medical condition, age, and location
* Secure and anonymous messaging for patients to share experiences and advice
* Discussion forums for patients to connect with others who have similar conditions
* Resource library with relevant articles, videos, and webinars
* Integration with wearable devices and electronic health records (EHRs) for personalized insights
* Moderation by medical professionals to ensure accuracy and validity of shared information

## Tech Stack
* Frontend:
  + React for building responsive and interactive user interfaces
  + Redux for state management
* Backend:
  + Node.js with Express.js for server-side logic and API connectivity
  + Passport.js for authentication and authorization
* Database:
  + MongoDB for storing patient data and discussion forums
  + PostgreSQL for storing sensitive medical information
* Infra/DevOps:
  + Docker for containerization
  + Kubernetes for orchestration and deployment
  + AWS for cloud hosting and scalability
* AI/ML:
  + Natural Language Processing (NLP) for sentiment analysis and topic modeling
  + Collaborative filtering for personalized matchmaking

## Architecture
MedMingle's architecture is designed as a microservices-based system, with separate services for authentication, matchmaking, discussion forums, and resource library. The frontend is built using React and communicates with the backend services through RESTful APIs. The backend services are deployed on a Kubernetes cluster, with MongoDB and PostgreSQL databases storing patient data and discussion forums. The system is hosted on AWS, ensuring scalability and high availability.
