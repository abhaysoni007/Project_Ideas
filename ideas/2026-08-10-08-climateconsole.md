# ClimateConsole
> Empowering sustainable cities through data-driven insights
## Problem 
The increasing concern of climate change has led cities and governments to focus on reducing their carbon footprint, but they often lack a unified platform to track, manage, and analyze their environmental impact. Current solutions are fragmented and do not provide real-time data visualization and analytics. This hinders the ability to make informed decisions and develop effective sustainability strategies.
## Target Users
* City council members and government officials
* Urban planners and sustainability managers
* Environmental scientists and researchers
* Citizens interested in tracking their city's environmental progress
## Key Features
* Real-time data ingestion from various sources such as IoT sensors, weather APIs, and government databases
* Interactive dashboards for visualizing carbon footprint, energy consumption, and waste management metrics
* Predictive analytics for forecasting environmental trends and identifying areas of improvement
* Collaborative tools for multi-stakeholder engagement and community outreach
* Integration with popular GIS mapping tools for spatial analysis and visualization
* Customizable reporting and alert systems for proactive decision-making
## Tech Stack
#### Frontend
* React with Material-UI for responsive and intuitive user interfaces
* D3.js for interactive data visualizations
#### Backend
* Node.js with Express.js for building scalable and secure APIs
* GraphQL for efficient data querying and schema management
#### Database
* PostgreSQL for storing and managing large datasets
* MongoDB for handling unstructured and semi-structured data
#### Infra/DevOps
* Docker for containerization and deployment
* Kubernetes for orchestration and scaling
* AWS for cloud infrastructure and services
#### AI/ML
* TensorFlow for building predictive models and machine learning algorithms
* Scikit-learn for data preprocessing and feature engineering
## Architecture
The ClimateConsole platform will follow a microservices architecture, with separate services for data ingestion, processing, and visualization. The frontend will be built using a modular and component-based approach, while the backend will utilize a service-oriented architecture for scalability and maintainability. The database will be designed for high availability and fault tolerance, with regular backups and disaster recovery procedures in place.
## Roadmap
* v1: Develop core features for data ingestion, visualization, and analytics, with a focus on energy consumption and carbon footprint tracking
* v2: Integrate predictive analytics and machine learning capabilities, with expanded support for waste management and transportation metrics
* v3: Enhance collaborative tools and community engagement features, with integration with popular social media platforms and citizen participation portals
