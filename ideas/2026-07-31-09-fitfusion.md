# FitFusion
> Unleash your fitness potential with personalized workouts and a supportive community
## Problem
The current fitness landscape is fragmented, with users having to juggle multiple apps for workout planning, tracking, and social sharing, leading to a lack of motivation and inconsistent progress. Many fitness apps also fail to provide personalized recommendations, neglecting users' unique goals and preferences. This results in a high dropout rate and unmet fitness aspirations.
## Target Users
* Fitness enthusiasts seeking structured workout plans and community support
* Individuals with specific fitness goals, such as weight loss or marathon training
* People looking for motivation and accountability in their fitness journey
## Key Features
* AI-powered workout planning based on user goals, fitness level, and preferences
* Social sharing of workout progress and achievements
* Community challenges and leaderboards to foster engagement and competition
* Personalized nutrition advice and meal planning integration
* Integration with popular wearables and fitness trackers
* Gamification elements, such as rewards and badges, to enhance motivation
## Tech Stack
### Frontend
* React Native for mobile app development
* Expo for streamlined development and deployment
### Backend
* Node.js with Express.js for API development
* GraphQL for efficient data querying and manipulation
### Database
* MongoDB for storing user data and workout plans
* Redis for caching and improving performance
### Infra/DevOps
* AWS for hosting and scalability
* Docker for containerization and deployment
### AI/ML
* TensorFlow.js for AI-powered workout planning and recommendations
## Architecture
The FitFusion app will follow a microservices architecture, with separate services for user management, workout planning, and social features. The frontend will communicate with the backend via GraphQL API, while the backend will interact with the database and AI/ML services as needed. This architecture will enable scalability, flexibility, and maintainability.
## Roadmap
* v1: Launch core features, including workout planning, social sharing, and community challenges
* v2: Integrate personalized nutrition advice, gamification elements, and wearables support
* v3: Enhance AI-powered workout planning with machine learning algorithms and expand community features to include forums and event planning
