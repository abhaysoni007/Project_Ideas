# GreenGrid
> Power your future, together.

## Problem
Homeowners with renewable installations often face barriers to sell excess energy back to the grid. Existing utility models lack transparency, dynamic pricing, and efficient peer‑to‑peer settlement, resulting in wasted resources and missed revenue opportunities.

## Target Users
- Homeowners with rooftop solar or wind systems
- Community energy cooperatives
- Local businesses with on‑site renewable generation
- Smart‑metered households interested in dynamic energy pricing
- Grid operators seeking demand‑side flexibility

## Key Features
- **P2P Energy Marketplace**: Real‑time trading between prosumers and consumers
- **Dynamic Pricing Engine**: Algorithmic price discovery based on supply, demand, and grid constraints
- **Blockchain‑Based Settlement**: Immutable smart contracts for transparent, auditable transactions
- **Demand Forecasting**: AI models predicting short‑term consumption and generation
- **Smart‑Meter Integration**: Unified API for meter data ingestion via MQTT/REST
- **Mobile & Web Apps**: Intuitive dashboards for monitoring and trading
- **Regulatory Compliance**: Automated reporting for local energy regulators

## Architecture
GreenGrid follows a microservice architecture with a dedicated marketplace, pricing, settlement, analytics, and device‑integration service. Services communicate via a Kafka event bus; the API gateway (Apollo GraphQL) aggregates data and enforces security. Edge devices publish meter data to MQTT, which the ingestion service forwards to TimescaleDB. A blockchain node (Ethereum or Polygon) handles settlement contracts, while an AI service exposes forecast endpoints. All components run in a containerized Kubernetes cluster managed by Terraform on AWS EKS, with automated CI/CD pipelines and observability stack integrated.
