# Finverge
> Empowering millennials and Gen Z to financially own their future
## Problem
Millennials and Gen Z adults often lack the financial literacy and planning skills to manage their money, investments, and career goals effectively, leading to financial stress and uncertainty. This can result in delayed financial independence and a lower quality of life. Existing financial planning tools often cater to older demographics, leaving a gap in the market for a platform tailored to younger adults.
## Target Users
* Millennials (born 1981-1996) and Gen Z adults (born 1997-2012)
* Individuals with a medium to high level of digital literacy
* Those seeking to improve their financial knowledge and planning skills
## Key Features
* Personalized financial planning and budgeting tools
* Investment tracking and portfolio analysis
* Career development resources and job market insights
* Community forums for discussion and support
* Integration with popular banking and investment platforms
* Gamification elements to encourage financial literacy and responsible money management
## Tech Stack
### Frontend
* React with Redux for state management
* Material-UI for responsive and accessible design
### Backend
* Node.js with Express.js for server-side logic
* GraphQL API for data querying and manipulation
### Database
* PostgreSQL for relational data storage
* MongoDB for NoSQL data storage and caching
### Infra/DevOps
* AWS for cloud hosting and scalability
* Docker for containerization and deployment
### AI/ML
* TensorFlow.js for client-side machine learning and predictive analytics
## Architecture
Finverge will follow a microservices architecture, with separate services for authentication, financial planning, investment tracking, and community features. This will enable scalability, flexibility, and easier maintenance, while also allowing for the integration of third-party services and APIs.
## Roadmap
* v1: Initial launch with basic financial planning and budgeting tools, investment tracking, and community forums
* v2: Addition of career development resources, job market insights, and gamification elements
* v3: Integration with popular banking and investment platforms, enhanced AI/ML-powered predictive analytics, and mobile app release
