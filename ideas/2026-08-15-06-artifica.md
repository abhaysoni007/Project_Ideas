# Artifica
> Revolutionizing digital art creation with AI-powered innovation
## Problem 
The process of creating unique digital art can be time-consuming and may require significant skill and experience. Artists and designers often struggle to produce high-quality, unique pieces that stand out in a crowded market. Furthermore, managing and selling these digital creations can be complicated and inefficient.

## Target Users 
* Professional artists and designers looking to expand their creative capabilities
* Emerging artists seeking to establish themselves in the digital art market
* Collectors and enthusiasts interested in purchasing unique digital art pieces

## Key Features 
* AI-generated art tool with customizable settings and prompts
* Digital art editor for refining and enhancing AI-created pieces
* Integrated marketplace for buying, selling, and trading digital art
* Support for creating and managing NFTs (non-fungible tokens) on popular blockchain platforms
* User profiles and portfolios for showcasing artwork and tracking sales
* Community features for discussing art, sharing techniques, and collaborating on projects

## Tech Stack 
* Frontend:
  + React for building the user interface and user experience
  + Redux for state management
* Backend:
  + Node.js with Express.js for handling server-side logic and API requests
  + GraphQL for querying and manipulating data
* Database:
  + PostgreSQL for storing user data, artwork, and transaction records
  + MongoDB for handling large files and metadata
* Infra/DevOps:
  + Docker for containerization and deployment
  + Kubernetes for orchestration and scaling
* AI/ML:
  + TensorFlow for building and training AI models
  + PyTorch for integrating with the frontend and backend

## Architecture 
The Artifica application will follow a microservices architecture, with separate services for the AI art generation, digital art editing, marketplace, and user management. This will allow for greater flexibility, scalability, and maintainability, as each service can be developed, updated, and deployed independently.

## Roadmap 
* v1:
  + Launch the AI art generation tool with basic customization options
  + Introduce the digital art editor with essential features
* v2:
  + Develop and integrate the marketplace for buying and selling digital art
  + Add support for NFT creation and management
* v3:
  + Enhance the AI art generation tool with advanced features and models
  + Implement community features and user profiles for showcasing artwork and tracking sales
