# FitForge
> Unleash your fitness potential, forge your strength
## Problem 
The current fitness tracking apps lack a social aspect, making it difficult for users to stay motivated and engaged in their fitness journey. Gym-goers and athletes need a platform that allows them to track their workouts, share progress, and compete with others in a fun and interactive way. This leads to a high dropout rate and a lack of accountability among fitness enthusiasts.

## Target Users
* Gym-goers
* Professional athletes
* Fitness enthusiasts
* Personal trainers and coaches
* Sports teams and groups

## Key Features
* Personalized workout tracking and analytics
* Social sharing and community features to connect with friends and like-minded individuals
* Leaderboards and challenges to foster competition and motivation
* Integration with popular fitness wearables and devices
* Customizable training plans and coaching advice from certified professionals
* In-app messaging and discussion forums for support and guidance

## Tech Stack
* Frontend:
  + ReactJS for building the user interface and user experience
  + Redux for state management
  + Material-UI for styling and design
* Backend:
  + Node.js with Express.js as the server-side framework
  + GraphQL API for querying and mutating data
  + Passport.js for authentication and authorization
* Database:
  + MongoDB for storing user data and workout records
  + Redis for caching and real-time updates
* Infra/DevOps:
  + Docker for containerization and deployment
  + Kubernetes for orchestration and scaling
  + AWS for cloud hosting and infrastructure
* AI/ML:
  + TensorFlow.js for building predictive models and personalized recommendations

## Architecture
The FitForge platform will follow a microservices architecture, with separate services for authentication, workout tracking, social features, and coaching advice. The frontend will be built using ReactJS and will communicate with the backend via GraphQL API. The backend will be responsible for handling business logic, integrating with third-party services, and storing data in the database.

## Roadmap
* v1:
  + Launch the minimum viable product (MVP) with core features and a basic user interface
  + Integrate with popular fitness wearables and devices
  + Establish a community of early adopters and gather feedback
* v2:
  + Enhance the user interface and user experience with Material-UI and responsive design
  + Introduce social sharing and community features to foster engagement and motivation
  + Develop a coaching advice feature with certified professionals and AI-powered personalized recommendations
* v3:
  + Launch a mobile app for iOS and Android devices
  + Integrate with popular health and fitness apps for a more comprehensive experience
  + Develop a premium subscription model with exclusive features and content
