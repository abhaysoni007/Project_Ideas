# HearthHub
> Your kitchen, your pantry, your personal chef  

## Problem (2-3 sentences)  
Health‑conscious cooks often struggle to make nutritious meals from the ingredients they already own. Existing recipe apps require manual ingredient entry and rarely account for seasonal produce or real‑time pantry data. This leads to food waste, wasted money, and suboptimal meal choices.  

## Target Users (bulleted)  
- Home cooks aged 25‑45 who prioritize healthy eating  
- Busy professionals needing quick, nutrient‑balanced meals  
- Families looking to reduce grocery bills and food waste  
- DIY grocery shoppers who maintain digital pantry lists  
- Tech‑savvy users who appreciate AI‑powered recommendations  

## Key Features (5-7 bullets)  
- Real‑time pantry sync via barcode scanning and grocery store APIs  
- Seasonal produce alerts and local farmer market integration  
- AI‑driven recipe suggestions optimized for nutrition, taste, and dietary restrictions  
- Meal‑planning calendar with automatic grocery list generation  
- Voice assistant integration (Alexa, Google Assistant) for hands‑free cooking  
- Smart shopping assistant that flags expired items and suggests substitutions  
- Community recipe sharing with peer‑reviewed nutrition metrics  

## Tech Stack (bullets grouped as Frontend, Backend, Database, Infra/DevOps, and AI/ML if relevant)  

### Frontend  
- React Native (iOS & Android) with Expo for rapid development  
- Next.js (React) for web portal  
- TypeScript, Redux Toolkit, Tailwind CSS  

### Backend  
- Node.js (v18) + Express + Apollo Server (GraphQL)  
- TypeScript, Prisma ORM  
- Dockerized services, orchestrated with Kubernetes  

### Database  
- PostgreSQL 15 (primary relational store)  
- Redis 7 (in‑memory cache & pub/sub for real‑time updates)  

### Infra/DevOps  
- GitHub Actions for CI/CD pipelines  
- Terraform scripts
