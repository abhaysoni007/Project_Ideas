# PulseTrack  
> Your health's early warning system

## Problem  
Chronic conditions like diabetes often progress silently until severe symptoms appear, leaving adults and caregivers with limited time to intervene. Existing wearables lack the predictive precision needed to flag early, subtle biometric changes. There is a pressing need for a proactive tool that continuously monitors and alerts users before a full-blown episode occurs.

## Target Users  
- Adults aged 35‑65 who want to prevent chronic illness  
- Caregivers monitoring loved ones with elevated risk  
- Primary care providers seeking actionable data  
- Health insurers and wellness programs aiming to reduce downstream costs  

## Key Features  
- Continuous biometric monitoring via wrist‑mounted sensors (heart rate, SpO₂, skin temperature, galvanic skin response)  
- Predictive analytics that flag early signs of glucose dysregulation and other chronic markers  
- Real‑time alerts (push, SMS, wearable vibration) with actionable recommendations  
- Secure, end‑to‑end encrypted data sync between wearable, mobile, and cloud  
- Personalized health insights dashboard for users and caregivers  
- Caregiver portal with role‑based access and shared alert history  
- Seamless integration with major EHR systems via HL7/FHIR APIs

## Architecture  
The wrist‑mounted device streams raw sensor data to the paired mobile app via BLE. The app buffers and forwards batches to the backend over HTTPS, where the data is ingested into TimescaleDB. A dedicated FastAPI microservice pulls the latest readings, runs them through a pre‑trained TensorFlow model, and determines risk scores. If a threshold is exceeded, the service pushes a notification to the user’s mobile app and updates the caregiver portal. All communications are TLS‑encrypted, and data at rest is encrypted with AWS KMS. Role‑based access controls protect sensitive health information.

## Roadmap  
- **v1**: MVP with wearable prototype, mobile app, basic heart‑rate & temperature monitoring, simple risk scoring, push notifications.  
- **v2**: Expand sensor suite (SpO₂, galvanic skin response), implement advanced ML models, caregiver portal, EHR integration via FHIR.  
- **v3**: Global rollout, regulatory certifications (FDA 510(k), CE), AI explainability dashboards, autoscaling infra for high‑volume analytics, multi‑language support.
