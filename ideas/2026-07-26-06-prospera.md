# Prospera
> Revolutionizing financial management for small businesses and entrepreneurs
## Problem 
The lack of access to affordable and personalized financial planning, accounting, and insurance services hinders the growth and success of small businesses and entrepreneurs. Traditional financial services are often costly and time-consuming, leaving many without the necessary support. This gap in the market can lead to poor financial decisions and increased risk of business failure.
## Target Users
* Small business owners (less than 50 employees)
* Entrepreneurs and solo founders
* Freelancers and independent contractors
## Key Features 
* Virtual AI assistant for basic financial queries and guidance
* Access to a network of certified financial experts for personalized advice
* Automated accounting and bookkeeping tools
* Customized insurance recommendations and policy management
* Integration with popular e-commerce and payment platforms
* Real-time financial reporting and analytics
## Tech Stack 
### Frontend
* React for building the user interface
* Material-UI for responsive design
### Backend
* Node.js with Express.js for server-side logic
* GraphQL API for data queries and mutations
### Database
* MongoDB for storing user data and financial records
### Infra/DevOps
* Docker for containerization
* Kubernetes for orchestration
* AWS for cloud hosting
### AI/ML
* TensorFlow for building the AI assistant's predictive models
* Natural Language Processing (NLP) for text-based user interactions
## Architecture 
Prospera's architecture is designed as a microservices-based system, with separate services for the AI assistant, accounting tools, and insurance recommendations. The frontend is built as a single-page application, communicating with the backend via the GraphQL API. The AI assistant is integrated with the backend services to provide personalized advice and guidance.
## Roadmap 
* v1: Launch the virtual AI assistant and basic accounting tools, with integration with popular e-commerce platforms
* v2: Introduce the network of certified financial experts and customized insurance recommendations
* v3: Enhance the AI assistant with predictive analytics and machine learning capabilities, and expand the platform to support additional financial services and integrations
