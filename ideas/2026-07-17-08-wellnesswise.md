# WellnessWise
> Empowering minds, one breath at a time
## Problem
Mental health issues are on the rise, and existing solutions often lack personalization and holistic approaches. Individuals and employers struggle to find effective ways to support mental wellness. This leads to decreased productivity, increased healthcare costs, and a lower quality of life.
## Target Users
* Individuals seeking personalized mental health support and wellness guidance
* Employers looking to provide mental health resources and improve employee well-being
* Healthcare professionals aiming to offer complementary therapies to their patients
## Key Features
* Personalized coaching plans based on user input and preferences
* Guided meditation sessions with experienced instructors
* Mood tracking and analytics to monitor progress and identify patterns
* Community forums for support and connection with peers
* Integration with popular wearable devices and health apps
* Customizable wellness plans for employers to support employee mental health
## Tech Stack
### Frontend
* React for building reusable UI components
* Redux for state management
* Material-UI for responsive design
### Backend
* Node.js with Express.js for server-side logic
* GraphQL API for querying and mutating data
* Passport.js for authentication and authorization
### Database
* MongoDB for storing user data and wellness plans
* PostgreSQL for storing analytics and mood tracking data
### Infra/DevOps
* AWS for hosting and scaling the application
* Docker for containerization and deployment
* Jenkins for continuous integration and delivery
### AI/ML
* TensorFlow for building predictive models and personalized recommendations
* Natural Language Processing (NLP) for sentiment analysis and mood tracking
## Architecture
The WellnessWise platform will follow a microservices architecture, with separate services for authentication, coaching, meditation, and mood tracking. This will allow for scalability, flexibility, and easier maintenance. The frontend will communicate with the backend via RESTful APIs and GraphQL queries.
## Roadmap
* v1: Develop and launch the core platform with personalized coaching, meditation, and mood tracking features
* v2: Integrate with wearable devices and health apps, add community forums and social sharing features
* v3: Implement AI-powered predictive models and NLP-driven sentiment analysis, expand employer offerings and analytics capabilities
