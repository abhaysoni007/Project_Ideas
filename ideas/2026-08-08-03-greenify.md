# Greenify
> Empowering a greener tomorrow, one upgrade at a time
## Problem
The increasing concern for environmental sustainability has led to a surge in demand for eco-friendly solutions, yet many homeowners and businesses struggle to identify and implement effective energy-efficient upgrades and sustainable practices. Current solutions often provide generic advice, failing to account for the unique needs and circumstances of each user. This lack of personalization hinders the widespread adoption of environmentally responsible habits.

## Target Users
* Homeowners seeking to reduce their energy consumption and environmental footprint
* Small to medium-sized businesses looking to improve their sustainability and reduce operational costs
* Environmentally conscious individuals interested in promoting sustainable practices within their communities

## Key Features
* Personalized energy audits and recommendations for energy-efficient upgrades
* Customizable sustainability plans tailored to each user's specific needs and goals
* Integration with popular smart home devices to track energy usage and provide real-time feedback
* Access to a community forum for sharing tips and best practices on sustainable living
* Gamification elements to encourage users to adopt and maintain environmentally friendly habits
* Partnerships with local businesses to offer exclusive discounts on energy-efficient products and services

## Tech Stack
* Frontend:
  + React for building the user interface
  + Redux for state management
  + Material-UI for styling and layout
* Backend:
  + Node.js with Express.js as the web framework
  + GraphQL API for handling queries and mutations
* Database:
  + MongoDB for storing user data and energy usage metrics
* Infra/DevOps:
  + Docker for containerization
  + Kubernetes for orchestration
  + AWS for cloud hosting and scaling
* AI/ML:
  + TensorFlow for building predictive models to forecast energy usage and provide personalized recommendations

## Architecture
The Greenify application will follow a microservices architecture, with separate services for handling user authentication, energy audits, and community features. The frontend will communicate with the backend via GraphQL API, while the backend will interact with the database and AI/ML models to generate personalized recommendations. This architecture will enable scalability, flexibility, and maintainability as the application grows and evolves.

## Roadmap
* v1:
  + Launch the minimum viable product (MVP) with core features and a limited user base
  + Gather feedback and iterate on the user experience
* v2:
  + Integrate with additional smart home devices and expand the community forum
  + Develop and deploy AI/ML models to improve the accuracy of energy usage forecasts and personalized recommendations
* v3:
  + Expand the platform to support businesses and organizations, with features such as customized sustainability plans and employee engagement tracking
  + Develop strategic partnerships with energy-efficient product manufacturers and service providers to offer exclusive discounts and promotions
