# Dreamscape
> Revolutionizing mental health therapy with immersive virtual reality experiences
## Problem
Mental health disorders such as anxiety, PTSD, and depression are increasingly prevalent, and traditional therapy methods often have limited effectiveness. Current virtual reality solutions for therapy are generic and lack personalization, failing to provide optimal treatment outcomes. This gap in personalized and immersive therapy solutions necessitates a new approach.
## Target Users
* Psychologists and therapists seeking innovative treatment methods
* Patients with mental health disorders requiring personalized therapy
* Researchers studying the effects of VR on mental health treatment
## Key Features
* Patient profiling and personalized experience generation
* Immersive VR environments for exposure therapy and cognitive behavioral therapy
* Real-time patient feedback and emotion tracking
* Integrated journaling and progress monitoring for patients
* Secure data storage and analytics for therapists and researchers
* Customizable treatment plans and VR scenarios
## Tech Stack
* Frontend:
  + Unity for VR experience development
  + React for web-based patient and therapist interfaces
* Backend:
  + Node.js with Express.js for server-side logic
  + GraphQL API for data exchange
* Database:
  + MongoDB for patient data and experience storage
* Infra/DevOps:
  + Docker for containerization
  + Kubernetes for deployment and scaling
* AI/ML:
  + TensorFlow for emotion recognition and predictive analytics
## Architecture
The Dreamscape system will consist of a Unity-based VR client, a React-based web interface, and a Node.js backend with a GraphQL API. Patient data will be stored in a MongoDB database, and the system will utilize Docker and Kubernetes for containerization and deployment. The AI/ML component will leverage TensorFlow for emotion recognition and predictive analytics, enabling personalized experience generation and adaptive treatment plans.
## Roadmap
* v1:
  + Develop core VR experience and patient profiling features
  + Implement basic therapist and patient interfaces
* v2:
  + Integrate real-time feedback and emotion tracking
  + Develop customizable treatment plans and VR scenarios
* v3:
  + Implement AI-driven predictive analytics and adaptive treatment plans
  + Expand platform to support multi-user environments and remote therapy sessions
