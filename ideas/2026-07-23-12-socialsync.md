# SocialSync
> Simplify social media management, amplify your impact
## Problem
Small businesses and non-profits often struggle to manage their social media presence, engage with their audiences, and measure the effectiveness of their campaigns. This can lead to wasted resources, missed opportunities, and a lack of online visibility. Effective social media management is crucial for their success and growth.
## Target Users
* Small business owners (less than 100 employees)
* Non-profit organization managers
* Social media managers
* Marketing teams
* Community managers
## Key Features
* Social media account integration (Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, LinkedIn)
* Content scheduling and posting
* Engagement tracking and analytics
* Campaign management and reporting
* Audience insights and demographic analysis
* Content suggestions and recommendations
## Tech Stack
### Frontend
* React.js
* Material-UI
* TypeScript
### Backend
* Node.js
* Express.js
* GraphQL
### Database
* MongoDB
* PostgreSQL
### Infra/DevOps
* AWS (EC2, S3, RDS)
* Docker
* Kubernetes
### AI/ML
* Natural Language Processing (NLP) for sentiment analysis
* Machine learning algorithms for predictive analytics
## Architecture
SocialSync will be built as a cloud-based, microservices-oriented application, with a scalable and secure architecture. The frontend will be a single-page application, built with React.js and Material-UI, while the backend will be a Node.js-based API, using Express.js and GraphQL. Data will be stored in a combination of MongoDB and PostgreSQL databases, and the application will be deployed on AWS, using Docker and Kubernetes for containerization and orchestration.
## Roadmap
* v1: Initial release with social media account integration, content scheduling, and basic analytics
* v2: Addition of engagement tracking, audience insights, and campaign management features
* v3: Integration of AI/ML-powered features, including NLP and predictive analytics, and advanced reporting capabilities
