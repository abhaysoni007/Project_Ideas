# EcoCycle
> Empowering a greener tomorrow, one recycle at a time.
## Problem
Households and businesses lack a comprehensive solution to monitor and minimize their waste management costs and environmental footprint. Current recycling and composting systems are often inefficient, leading to unnecessary waste and increased expenses. This results in a significant negative impact on the environment and a substantial economic burden.
## Target Users
* Households seeking to reduce their environmental impact and lower waste management bills
* Small to medium-sized businesses aiming to enhance their sustainability credentials and cut costs
* Local authorities and waste management companies looking to optimize recycling and composting processes
## Key Features
* Personalized waste management plans based on user input and behavior
* Real-time tracking of waste generation and recycling efficiency
* Automated composting schedules and reminders
* Integrated online community for sharing eco-friendly tips and best practices
* Customizable alerts and notifications for waste collection and recycling deadlines
* Data analytics and visualization tools to monitor progress and identify areas for improvement
## Tech Stack
#### Frontend
* React for building responsive and interactive user interfaces
* Material-UI for consistent and accessible design
#### Backend
* Node.js with Express.js for scalable and efficient server-side logic
* GraphQL API for flexible and performant data querying
#### Database
* MongoDB for storing and retrieving user data and waste management records
* PostgreSQL for handling complex queries and data analytics
#### Infra/DevOps
* Docker for containerization and deployment
* Kubernetes for orchestration and scaling
* AWS for cloud hosting and infrastructure management
#### AI/ML
* TensorFlow for developing predictive models and optimizing waste management recommendations
## Architecture
The EcoCycle system will consist of a web application built with React, a Node.js backend with GraphQL API, and a MongoDB/PostgreSQL database. The application will be containerized using Docker and deployed on AWS using Kubernetes for orchestration. TensorFlow will be integrated for predictive modeling and optimization of waste management recommendations.
## Roadmap
* v1: Initial launch with basic features, including personalized waste management plans and real-time tracking
* v2: Introduction of automated composting schedules, integrated online community, and customizable alerts
* v3: Advanced data analytics and visualization tools, along with AI-powered predictive modeling and optimization of waste management recommendations
