# MedMingle
> Connecting patients, empowering lives
## Problem
Patients with rare or chronic medical conditions often feel isolated and lack a support system, leading to decreased mental and emotional well-being. Existing online forums may not cater specifically to their needs, making it difficult for them to find relevant resources and communities. This can exacerbate feelings of loneliness and frustration.
## Target Users
* Patients diagnosed with chronic or rare medical conditions
* Family members and caregivers of patients
* Medical professionals seeking to provide emotional support and resources to patients
## Key Features
* User profiles with condition-specific information and support group subscriptions
* Online forums and discussion boards for patients to share experiences and advice
* Video conferencing for virtual support group meetings and events
* Resource library with relevant articles, webinars, and expert interviews
* Private messaging system for one-on-one support and connections
* Integration with wearable devices and health tracking apps for data sharing and insights
## Tech Stack
Frontend:
* React with Redux for state management
* Material-UI for responsive and accessible design
Backend:
* Node.js with Express.js for RESTful API development
* Passport.js for authentication and authorization
Database:
* MongoDB for storing user data and forum discussions
Infra/DevOps:
* AWS for hosting and scalability
* Docker for containerization and deployment
AI/ML:
* Natural Language Processing (NLP) for sentiment analysis and content moderation
## Architecture
MedMingle's architecture will follow a microservices pattern, with separate services for user management, forum discussions, and resource library. The frontend will communicate with these services through RESTful APIs, ensuring a scalable and maintainable system. Data will be stored in a MongoDB database, with AWS providing hosting and scalability.
## Roadmap
* v1: Develop core features, including user profiles, online forums, and resource library, with a basic design and limited scalability
* v2: Implement video conferencing, private messaging, and wearable device integration, with improved design and enhanced scalability
* v3: Integrate NLP for sentiment analysis and content moderation, and expand the resource library with expert interviews and webinars, with a focus on AI-driven personalized support and recommendations
