# Sonder
> Empowering mental wellness, one conversation at a time.
## Problem
Mental health conditions like anxiety and depression affect millions of people worldwide, often with limited access to personalized support and coaching. Existing solutions are often generic, not tailored to individual needs, and may not be engaging or accessible. This can lead to poor treatment outcomes and decreased quality of life.
## Target Users
* Individuals struggling with anxiety, depression, or other mental health conditions
* Mental health professionals seeking to provide personalized support to their clients
* Organizations looking to offer employee wellness programs with a focus on mental health
## Key Features
* Personalized coaching plans based on user-inputted goals and progress tracking
* AI-driven chatbot for emotional support and guidance
* Mood tracking and emotional state analysis
* Access to a library of mindfulness exercises and meditation techniques
* Integration with popular wearable devices and health trackers
* Secure messaging for communication with licensed therapists
## Tech Stack
#### Frontend
* React Native for mobile app development
* Redux for state management
* Expo for streamlined development and deployment
#### Backend
* Node.js with Express.js for server-side logic
* GraphQL API for data querying and mutation
* Passport.js for authentication and authorization
#### Database
* MongoDB for storing user data and chat logs
* Redis for caching and real-time data processing
#### Infra/DevOps
* AWS for cloud infrastructure and hosting
* Docker for containerization and deployment
* CircleCI for continuous integration and testing
#### AI/ML
* TensorFlow.js for client-side machine learning
* Natural Language Processing (NLP) with spaCy for text analysis
## Architecture
The Sonder app will follow a microservices architecture, with separate services for the chatbot, coaching plans, and user data management. The frontend will communicate with the backend via GraphQL API, and the AI/ML models will be integrated into the chatbot and coaching plan services. This architecture will allow for scalability, flexibility, and maintainability.
## Roadmap
* v1: Develop and launch the core features, including personalized coaching plans, mood tracking, and AI-driven chatbot
* v2: Integrate with wearable devices and health trackers, add support for secure messaging with licensed therapists
* v3: Expand the AI/ML capabilities to include predictive analytics and personalized recommendations for mindfulness exercises and meditation techniques
