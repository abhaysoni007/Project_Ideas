# VerbaLink
> Unlock the power of customer conversations with AI-driven insights
## Problem
 Businesses struggle to extract valuable insights from customer interactions, leading to missed opportunities and poor customer experiences. Current solutions are often manual, time-consuming, and lacking in depth. This results in a significant gap between customer expectations and business understanding.
## Target Users
* Customer service teams
* Sales teams
* Marketing teams
* Product managers
* Business analysts
## Key Features
* Conversation transcription and summarization
* Sentiment analysis and emotional intelligence tracking
* Topic modeling and trend identification
* Real-time alerts and notifications for critical customer issues
* Integration with popular CRM and helpdesk systems
* Customizable dashboards and reporting
## Tech Stack
#### Frontend
* React with Material-UI for a responsive and intuitive interface
* Webpack for module bundling and optimization
#### Backend
* Node.js with Express.js for a scalable and performant server-side architecture
* GraphQL for flexible and efficient API design
#### Database
* MongoDB for storing and querying large amounts of conversational data
* Redis for caching and real-time data processing
#### Infra/DevOps
* Docker for containerization and deployment
* Kubernetes for orchestration and scaling
* AWS for cloud infrastructure and services
#### AI/ML
* spaCy for natural language processing and entity recognition
* TensorFlow for machine learning model development and training
## Architecture
VerbaLink's architecture is designed as a microservices-based system, with separate services for transcription, sentiment analysis, and topic modeling. The frontend is built as a single-page application, communicating with the backend via GraphQL APIs. The backend services are containerized and orchestrated using Docker and Kubernetes, with MongoDB and Redis providing data storage and caching.
## Roadmap
* v1: Develop core conversation transcription and sentiment analysis features, with integration with popular CRM systems
* v2: Add topic modeling and trend identification capabilities, with customizable dashboards and reporting
* v3: Introduce real-time alerts and notifications, with advanced AI/ML-powered insights and recommendations
