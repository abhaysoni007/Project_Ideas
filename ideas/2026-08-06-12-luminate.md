# Luminate
> Empowering non-profits to shine brighter through data-driven fundraising
## Problem 
Non-profits and social enterprises often struggle to effectively manage their fundraising efforts and engage with donors due to limited resources and lack of data insights. This can lead to missed opportunities, inefficient allocation of resources, and ultimately, a negative impact on their social mission. Existing solutions are often costly, cumbersome, or inadequate for their needs.
## Target Users
* Non-profit organizations
* Social enterprises
* Fundraising teams
* Donor engagement specialists
* Grant writers
## Key Features
* Customizable fundraising campaign management
* Donor segmentation and profiling
* Real-time data analytics and insights
* Automated reporting and dashboard visualization
* Integration with popular CRM systems
* Secure online donation processing
## Tech Stack
* Frontend:
  + React
  + Redux
  + Material-UI
* Backend:
  + Node.js
  + Express
  + GraphQL
* Database:
  + PostgreSQL
  + MongoDB
* Infra/DevOps:
  + AWS
  + Docker
  + Kubernetes
* AI/ML:
  + TensorFlow
  + Scikit-learn
## Architecture
Luminate will be built as a modular, microservices-based platform, with a separate service for each key feature, allowing for scalability, flexibility, and maintainability. The frontend will be a single-page application, while the backend will consist of multiple services communicating via GraphQL APIs.
## Roadmap
* v1:
  + Initial platform launch with core features
  + Integration with popular CRM systems
* v2:

  + Automated reporting and dashboard visualization
* v3:
