# Lumina
> Empowering minds, illuminating futures
## Problem 
Mental health issues such as anxiety and stress are increasingly prevalent among teenagers and young adults, often due to lack of access to supportive resources and safe spaces for open discussion. Traditional therapy methods can be intimidating or inconvenient, leading to unaddressed emotional challenges. This can have severe long-term consequences on mental wellbeing and overall quality of life.

## Target Users
* Teenagers (13-19 years old) experiencing anxiety, stress, or other emotional challenges
* Young adults (20-30 years old) seeking mental health support and coping strategies
* Parents, educators, or counselors looking for resources to support the mental health of teenagers and young adults

## Key Features
* Conversational AI-powered chatbot for immediate support and emotional intake
* Immersive virtual reality therapy sessions tailored to individual needs and goals
* Personalized coping strategies and mindfulness exercises
* Access to a community forum for peer support and connection
* Integration with popular calendar apps for scheduling therapy sessions
* Progress tracking and analytics for users and authorized supporters (e.g., parents, counselors)

## Tech Stack
* Frontend: 
  + React for web and mobile application development
  + A-Frame for virtual reality experiences
* Backend: 
  + Node.js with Express.js framework
  + GraphQL API for data management and queries
* Database: 
  + MongoDB for storing user data and therapy session records
* Infra/DevOps: 
  + AWS for cloud infrastructure and scalability
  + Docker for containerization and deployment
* AI/ML: 
  + TensorFlow.js for client-side AI model integration
  + Google Cloud AI Platform for training and deploying machine learning models

## Architecture
The Lumina platform will follow a microservices architecture, with separate services for the chatbot, virtual reality therapy, community forum, and user management. This design allows for flexibility, scalability, and easier maintenance, ensuring a seamless and secure user experience across all features and platforms.

## Roadmap
* v1: 
  + Develop and deploy the AI-powered chatbot with basic coping strategies and emotional intake
  + Establish partnerships with mental health organizations for content validation and support
* v2: 
  + Introduce virtual reality therapy sessions with initial scenarios and environments
  + Implement community forum for peer support and connection
* v3: 
  + Enhance AI model with machine learning capabilities for personalized therapy recommendations
  + Expand virtual reality therapy sessions with more diverse and interactive scenarios
