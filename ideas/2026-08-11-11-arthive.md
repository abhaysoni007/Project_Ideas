# ArtHive
> Connecting creators, curators, and collectors in a vibrant art ecosystem
## Problem
The art market is fragmented, making it difficult for artists to gain visibility, galleries to reach a broader audience, and collectors to discover new talent. Existing platforms often focus on a single aspect, such as online marketplaces or social networks, without providing a comprehensive experience. This leads to a lack of engagement and limited opportunities for all stakeholders.
## Target Users
* Emerging and established artists seeking to showcase and sell their work
* Galleries and art institutions looking to expand their audience and manage sales
* Collectors and enthusiasts interested in discovering new art and connecting with creators
## Key Features
* Artist profiles with portfolios and exhibition history
* Gallery and exhibition listings with event calendars and sales catalogs
* Online marketplace for buying and selling art, including secure payment processing
* Social networking features for users to follow, share, and discuss art
* Personalized recommendations based on user interests and preferences
* Integration with popular social media platforms for seamless sharing
## Tech Stack
* Frontend:
  + React for building responsive and interactive user interfaces
  + Redux for state management and data flow
* Backend:
  + Node.js with Express.js for building scalable and secure APIs
  + GraphQL for efficient data querying and schema management
* Database:
  + MongoDB for storing and retrieving user data, art listings, and sales information
  + Redis for caching and optimizing database queries
* Infra/DevOps:
  + AWS for cloud hosting, scalability, and reliability
  + Docker for containerization and deployment
* AI/ML:
  + TensorFlow for building recommendation models and predictive analytics
## Architecture
The ArtHive platform will follow a microservices architecture, with separate services for user management, art listings, marketplace, and social networking. This will enable flexible development, scaling, and maintenance of individual components, while ensuring a cohesive and seamless user experience.
## Roadmap
* v1: Launch core features, including artist profiles, gallery listings, and online marketplace, with a basic social networking component
* v2: Introduce personalized recommendations, integration with social media platforms, and enhanced search functionality
* v3: Develop and integrate AI-powered features, such as predictive analytics and automated art curation, to further enhance user engagement and platform value proposition
