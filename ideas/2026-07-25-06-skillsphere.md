# SkillSphere
> Empowering professionals to showcase their skills and connect with opportunities
## Problem 
The traditional resume and portfolio are no longer sufficient for professionals to showcase their skills and experience, and finding relevant job openings and training opportunities can be a time-consuming and frustrating process. Many professionals struggle to effectively demonstrate their capabilities, leading to missed opportunities. This results in a significant gap between professionals and the opportunities they deserve.
## Target Users
* Professionals looking to advance their careers
* Recruiters and hiring managers seeking top talent
* Training and education providers offering courses and certifications
## Key Features
* Interactive portfolio builder with multimedia uploads and customizable templates
* AI-powered job matching and recommendations
* Personalized learning pathways and training suggestions
* Social networking features for connecting with peers and industry leaders
* Integrated messaging and application system for job openings
* User reviews and ratings for training providers and job openings
## Tech Stack
* Frontend:
  + React for building interactive and immersive user interfaces
  + Redux for state management
* Backend:
  + Node.js with Express.js for building scalable and secure API endpoints
  + GraphQL for querying and mutating data
* Database:
  + MongoDB for storing user data and portfolio information
  + PostgreSQL for storing job openings and training opportunities
* Infra/DevOps:
  + AWS for hosting and deploying the application
  + Docker for containerization and orchestration
* AI/ML:
  + TensorFlow.js for building and deploying machine learning models
## Architecture
The SkillSphere application will follow a microservices architecture, with separate services for the frontend, backend, and AI/ML components. The frontend will communicate with the backend via RESTful APIs, while the backend will interact with the database and AI/ML services as needed. This will allow for scalability, flexibility, and ease of maintenance.
## Roadmap
* v1:
  + Launch basic portfolio builder and job matching features
  + Integrate with popular job boards and training providers
* v2:
  + Add social networking features and user reviews/ratings
  + Introduce personalized learning pathways and training suggestions
* v3:
  + Implement advanced AI-powered job matching and recommendations
  + Expand to new industries and job categories, including freelance and contract work
